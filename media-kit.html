Body Blurb (150 words — ready to publish)

Lever AI and ProPlum Network have released a free, web-based Plumbing Job Cost Calculator designed to bridge the gap between homeowner expectations and contractor estimates. The tool synthesizes regional labor rates and localized material baselines to generate realistic pricing ranges for common residential repairs — water heater replacements, whole-home repiping, sewer line diagnostics, and fixture installations.

"The calculator serves as an educational benchmark, not a binding estimate," says founder Jeff Mann. "It helps homeowners budget realistically and helps plumbers avoid the tire-kicker calls that waste dispatch time."

The tool requires no account, no email, and no paywall. Contractors can request embed code to add the calculator directly to their own websites.

Try it: https://www.lever-ai.app/faq.html

The "Carbon Paper Quoting" Problem

Most plumbers still quote like it's 1985 — scribbling numbers on carbon paper, hoping the customer doesn't flinch at the price. The problem? Homeowners with zero baseline knowledge call three plumbers, pick the lowest bid, and the pro with fair pricing loses to the guy cutting corners. The calculator fixes this by educating the homeowner before the first phone call, so they understand why a water heater replacement costs $1,200-$2,800 before they ever dial a number.
