# Lever AI

**AI Search Visibility Platform for Multi-Location Brands**

Lever AI provides infrastructure and intelligence for franchise networks, multi-location service brands, and enterprise companies to establish and maintain visibility in AI-powered search platforms — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and emerging AI search engines.

## What We Do

Traditional SEO optimizes for Google crawlers. AI Search Visibility optimizes for AI synthesis engines that don't browse websites — they consume structured data, citations, and semantic content to generate answers.

### Core Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **AI Visibility Audit** | Comprehensive 8-point scoring across all locations. Identifies ghost pages, missing schema, and technical debt. |
| **Schema Deployment** | Network-wide deployment of LocalBusiness schema, `llms.txt`, and structured data — not just the homepage. |
| **Competitor Intelligence** | Track competitor AI visibility, answer capture rates, and threat positioning across markets. |
| **Ongoing Monitoring** | Monthly board-ready reports, visibility change alerts, and optimization recommendations. |
