## 🎯 OBJECTION HANDLING

**"We're not interested in third-party tools."**
> "Totally get it. I'm not asking you to endorse anything. Just thought your contractors might appreciate a free resource. Most branch managers I talk to are always looking for something fresh to share at their weekly meeting. If it's not useful, no harm done."

**"We already have pricing resources."**
> "This one's different — it's built for homeowners, not contractors. The plumber doesn't have to do anything. It pre-educates the customer before they ever dial. That's less phone tag, more booked jobs."

**"I'm busy, send me an email."**
> "Absolutely. What's the best email for you? I'll send a one-pager with the live link. Takes 30 seconds to check out. And if it's not useful for your next meeting, just delete it."

**"We don't share vendor tools."**
> "Fair enough. I'm not a vendor — I'm just a guy who built something free because I got tired of seeing good plumbers lose jobs to price shoppers. But I respect your process. If you change your mind, the tool's at lever-ai.app. Have a good one."
