INTRODUCTION:
Over 31% of U.S. homeowners now use AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) to find local plumbers. If your business isn't structured for AI discovery, you're invisible to a growing segment of high-intent customers.

This checklist helps plumbing contractors audit their AI search visibility in under 10 minutes. Complete all 8 checkpoints to ensure AI search engines recommend YOUR business when homeowners ask for help.

---

CHECKLIST:

☐ 1. AI Crawler Access
Your robots.txt file should NOT block GPTBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, or Bingbot. These are the crawlers used by AI search engines. If blocked, your site is invisible to AI.

How to check: Visit yoursite.com/robots.txt and verify none of these user-agents are disallowed.

☐ 2. Google Business Profile Optimization
- Business category set to "Plumber" (primary)
- Service areas defined (up to 20 cities/suburbs)
- 50+ photos uploaded (jobs, team, trucks)
- Business description includes services + service areas
- Regular posts (at least 1 per week)
- Review response rate: 100%

☐ 3. LocalBusiness Schema on Every Page
Every service page and location page needs JSON-LD structured data declaring:
- Business name and type (@type: Plumber)
- Address with geo coordinates
- Phone number
- Service area
- Hours of operation
- Price range

Without this schema, AI search engines cannot confidently associate your business with local plumbing queries.

☐ 4. Review Velocity (3+ per Week)
Google weights recency heavily. A competitor with 500 old reviews looks stagnant compared to your 50 recent reviews.

Target: 3-5 new Google reviews per week minimum.
Pro tip: Send SMS review requests 30-60 minutes after job completion when satisfaction is highest.

☐ 5. Answer-First Content Architecture
AI search engines extract "consensus passages" — direct answers to questions. Structure your service pages to lead with the answer, then provide supporting details.

Example (good):
"Water heater replacement in Tampa typically costs $1,200-$3,500 depending on tank type, location, and permit requirements. Here's what factors into the price..."

Example (bad):
"Water heaters are an essential part of every home. Since 1998, we've been serving the Tampa Bay area with quality plumbing services..."

☐ 6. NAP Consistency Across the Web
Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be IDENTICAL across:
- Your website
- Google Business Profile
- Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor
- Facebook, Nextdoor
- Local directories

Even small differences ("St." vs "Street", "Suite 100" vs "#100") fragment AI entity recognition.

☐ 7. Emergency Response Signals
Homeowners searching at 11 PM for "emergency plumber" need instant confirmation you handle after-hours calls.

Your website must clearly display:
- "24/7 Emergency Service" above the fold
- Emergency phone number (different from office line)
- Average response time (e.g., "45 minutes or less")
- Service area map

☐ 8. AI Search Testing
Manually test your visibility:
- Ask ChatGPT: "Who's the best plumber in [Your City]?"
- Ask Perplexity: "Emergency plumber near me in [Your City]"
- Check Google AI Overviews for "plumber [Your City]"

If you're not mentioned, your AI visibility needs work.
