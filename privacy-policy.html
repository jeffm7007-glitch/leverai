Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Lever AI LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered emergency dispatch and virtual receptionist services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our AI dispatch services:

  • Account Information: Business name, contact details, service area, and license information.
  • Call & Message Data: Voice recordings, transcriptions, SMS content, caller phone numbers, and call metadata.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs.
  • Emergency Data: Caller addresses, problem descriptions, and urgency classifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Answer and route emergency calls through our AI dispatcher (Sam)
  • Send text alerts to on-call contractors
  • Improve AI response accuracy and emergency classification
  • Generate call analytics and performance reports
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. AI Processing & Data Retention

Call recordings and transcriptions may be processed by third-party AI services (OpenRouter, Telnyx) to enable real-time emergency classification. We retain call data for:

  • Quality assurance: 90 days
  • Legal compliance: 7 years (where required)
  • AI training (anonymized): Indefinite

5. Information Sharing

We share information only as necessary:

  • Contractors: Emergency details are shared with the assigned plumber/HVAC technician
  • Service Providers: Telnyx (telephony), Netlify (hosting), OpenRouter (AI processing)
  • Legal: When required by subpoena or to protect safety

6. Data Security

We employ enterprise-grade security: TLS 1.3 encryption, SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, role-based access controls, and regular penetration testing.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of call recordings (subject to legal holds)
  • Opt out of AI training data usage
  • Receive a copy of your data

Contact: jeff@proplumnetwork.com

8. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for licensed contractors and homeowners over 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
